How to Avoid ISP Throttling

Internet providers sometimes slow down streaming traffic. This can cause buffering. SubTV IP recommends two simple tweaks:

  1. Use Cloudflare DNS – set your DNS to 1.1.1.1 on your router or device.
  2. Increase buffer / cache to 5,000 ms – this setting is available in most IPTV players. It holds a few seconds of video ahead, so short network dips don't interrupt playback.

These tips help keep your stream smooth even on busy connections.
